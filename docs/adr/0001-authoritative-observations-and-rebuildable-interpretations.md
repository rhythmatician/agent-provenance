# Keep observations authoritative and interpretations rebuildable

The append-only event stream is the historical authority. Deterministic derivations, semantic inferences, claim assessments, and future graph edges are disposable projections that retain provenance links to their source events and the versioned rule that produced them. An `Observation Gap` is itself an authoritative observation, so incomplete capture remains visible instead of being converted into false certainty.
